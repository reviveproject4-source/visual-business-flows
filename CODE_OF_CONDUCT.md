# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive when discussing different viewpoints.
- Accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing feedback on the work, not the person.
- Considering the operational context and experience of others.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without permission, and sustained disruptive conduct.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project communication channels.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer at **tri.raida.consulting@gmail.com**. Include relevant links or context. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and documented where appropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
